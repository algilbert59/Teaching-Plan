Learning objectives
Write SMART, measurable objectives using Bloom's Taxonomy. Include objectives from all three learning domains when appropriate.
SMART objectives: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Realistic, Time-bound. Begin with "By the end of this session, the learner will be able to..."
Action verbs by domain — Cognitive: state, identify, explain, describe, compare, apply, analyze  |  Psychomotor: perform, demonstrate, administer, prepare, show  |  Affective: express, verbalize, commit, comply, acknowledge, value
Bloom's Taxonomy (revised): Remember → Understand → Apply → Analyze → Evaluate → Create
Most patient teaching objectives target the Apply level or below. Psychomotor and affective domains are equally important for safe self-care.

Evaluation of learning
How will you determine whether each learning objective has been met? Plan your evaluation strategy before teaching begins.
Teach-back is the gold standard. Ask the learner to explain or demonstrate — never ask "Do you understand?" or "Does that make sense?" Open-ended teach-back prompts reveal true comprehension. Plan at least one teach-back question per objective.
